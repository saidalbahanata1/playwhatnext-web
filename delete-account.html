Delete your What Next account

You can request deletion of your What Next account (developer: Said Al Bahanata) and all data associated with it at any time.

How to request deletion

Send an email to playwhatnext@gmail.com with the subject “Delete my account” from the email address linked to your account (or include your in-game player name if you signed in as a guest).

What gets deleted

  • Your account (Google sign-in or email login)
  • Your cloud save: game progress, coins, items and settings
  • Your leaderboard entry
  • Gameplay analytics events linked to your account
  • Any bug reports you submitted, including screenshots

Deletion is permanent and completed within 30 days of your request. We will confirm by reply once it is done.

Partial deletion

If you only want your cloud save wiped but wish to keep the account, say so in your email and we will reset the save instead.

See also our privacy policy. Purchases are processed by Google Play; refund requests follow Google Play’s refund policy.
