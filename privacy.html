Privacy Policy — What Next

Last updated: August 22, 2026

What Next is a casual mini-game collection. This policy explains what data the game handles.

Data we collect

  • Anonymous account ID. The app creates a random anonymous account (via Supabase) to sync your progress. No name, email, phone number, or other identity is required or collected.
  • Game progress. Your save data (levels completed, in-game currency and items, settings, and the display name/avatar you optionally choose in the game) is stored on your device and synced to our backend so your progress survives reinstalls.
  • Leaderboard data. Your chosen display name, avatar, and levels-completed count may be shown to other players on the leaderboard.
  • Bug reports (optional). If you choose to send a bug report, we receive your written remarks, an optional screenshot you attach, your app version, and your device model/OS version.

What we do NOT collect

  • No precise location, contacts, camera, microphone, or files access.
  • No advertising identifiers, and no third-party advertising or analytics SDKs. Ad placements inside the game are simulated for demonstration only.
  • No selling or sharing of data with third parties.

Where data is stored

Synced data is stored with Supabase (our backend provider). Local save data stays on your device and is removed when you uninstall the app.

Data deletion

To delete your synced data (save, leaderboard entry, and any bug reports), contact us with your in-game display name at the email below, or simply uninstall the app to remove all local data.

Children

The game is suitable for general audiences, contains no real gambling, and offers no real-money prizes. Simulated in-game purchases and ads exist for demonstration only in this prototype.
